About CivilGate
Why does this exist?
Most of us have a vague feeling that something is off — that decisions are being made that will affect our lives, but it’s hard to know what’s actually happening. CivilGate tracks government policy across the EU, US and UK and scores its real-world impact, so you can see what’s changing and where things are heading.
What do the scores mean?
Each policy gets a score from −10 to +10 across five dimensions: Social, Environmental, Economic, Human Rights, and Governance. 0 means no meaningful effect. ±5 means significant real-world impact. ±9–10 means transformative or landmark change.
How do we know this is accurate?
All data comes directly from official government sources: US Congress (congress.gov), EUR-Lex (EU legislation register), and UK Parliament. Summaries and impact scores are generated by AI based on the actual policy text — not headlines or press releases. Updated daily.
